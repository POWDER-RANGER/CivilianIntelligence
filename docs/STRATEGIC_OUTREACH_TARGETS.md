## Qualification rubric

Before spending time on any prospect, verify:

1. **Problem fit:** Can you name one workflow they currently sell or operate that CIVINTELLIGENCE could materially improve?
2. **Economic buyer:** Is there a product leader, business-unit GM, founder, or corp-dev contact who can sponsor the discussion?
3. **Rights fit:** Would the MIT license and source/data terms support the partnership they need?
4. **Proof threshold:** What production acceptance, security, source freshness, and user evidence would they require?
5. **Path to value:** Is the realistic next step a paid pilot, integration, licensing/support agreement, investment, or acquisition?

Prioritize conversations where the buyer can articulate a measurable use case and an internal owner. A generic positive response is not evidence of a valuation.
