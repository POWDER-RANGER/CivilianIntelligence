Public-interest technology · Creator and media briefing

See public information in context.

CIVINTELLIGENCE is an evolving public-information exploration platform built to make geographic context and publicly accessible data easier to examine. We invite independent journalists, First Amendment auditors, civic researchers and accountability creators to explore it critically.

Open live platformReview creator briefing

The live app may take additional time to wake on free-tier hosting. Data coverage and availability can vary.

01 / Purpose

Mission statement

Help the public investigate civic and geographic information with clearer context, transparent limitations and independent judgment. CIVINTELLIGENCE aims to support public awareness and accountability—not to replace verification, reporting or due process.
02 / What to explore

Platform scope

Geographic exploration

Use the map-based interface to inspect places and geographic context relevant to public-interest research.

Public-data discovery

Explore available data layers and information sources, while checking their source, timestamp and coverage in the live application.

Independent verification

Evaluate where the application is useful, where data is missing and which claims require primary-source confirmation.

Feature availability is subject to the live deployment. This briefing does not promise real-time, complete or authoritative coverage of any feed.

03 / For auditors, journalists and creators

A practical review guide

  1. Open the live application. Test it on desktop or mobile, allowing for a possible cold start.
  2. Choose a familiar location. Try geographic exploration with an area you know well.
  3. Check the evidence. Inspect any available metadata and compare material facts against primary public sources.
  4. Report limitations. Note stale data, unavailable layers, performance issues or confusing presentation.
  5. Share an independent assessment. Positive and critical coverage are both welcome; do not present this portal as endorsement or sponsorship.
